3
Enter your password
Enter your password. The default initial password is $eastar12.
4
Click the Login button
Click the red Login button.
5
Change initial password (first login only)

03 · Stock Status
How Much Stock Do We Have?
Check which forms are available and how many are in stock at your branch. Color-coded status indicators let you see the situation at a glance.
1
Left menu → Click Stock Status
After logging in, click Stock Status in the left sidebar.
2
Read the color-coded stock status
OK Green: Stock is sufficient.
Low Yellow: Stock is below the minimum threshold.
Empty Red: Stock is at 0.
3
Use search to find items quickly
Type a form name in the search box to filter the list instantly.
Administrators can view stock for all branches. Branch staff can only see their own branch.

2
Select the form type

3

07 · Transfer
Sending Forms to Another Branch
Use this when another branch needs forms or when you need to receive forms from another branch. The request must be approved by the receiving branch before it is completed.
🔵 Requesting a Transfer (Sending to Another Branch)
1
Left menu → Click Transfer
Click Transfer under the Inventory section.
2
Select the destination branch
Select the branch to send forms to from the dropdown. (e.g. GMP, CJU)
3
Enter form and quantity, then submit

Check the notification badge

11 · Transport Form Request
Requesting Transport Forms
When headquarters opens a transport form request period, you can submit requests for the forms your branch needs.
1
Left menu → Click Transport Form Request
Click Transport Form Request under the Transport Form section.
2
Check the application period
Requests are not accepted outside the application period. The period is set by the administrator and displayed on the page.
3
Enter required forms and quantities
Enter the quantity needed for each form. Current stock and recent usage are shown alongside for reference.
4
Submit the request
Click the Submit Request button. The administrator will review and either approve or reject it.
5
Check the result
You can check the approval/rejection result in My Request History in the left menu. You will also be notified by email when a decision is made.
Requests cannot be submitted after the application period has ended. Always check the dates!

12 · Admin Only
Admin Functions
Visible only when logged in with an administrator account
In addition to all branch staff features, administrators can manage users, process requests, and configure application periods.
👥 User Management
Create and delete staff accounts. New accounts are automatically assigned the initial password $eastar12, and users are forced to change it on first login. Resetting a password also reverts it to $eastar12.
Menu: Admin → User Management
📋 Transport Form Request Processing
Approve or reject transport form requests submitted by branches. The result is communicated to the branch by email.
Menu: Admin → Transport Form Request Management
📅 Transport Form Application Period
Set the period during which transport form requests are accepted. Branches cannot submit requests outside of this period.
Menu: Admin → Application Period Settings
🗓️ Transport Item Application Period
Set the period during which catalog supply requests are accepted. Outside this period, adding items to the cart and submitting requests will both be blocked.
Menu: Admin → Transport Item Application Period
✉️ Branch Email Management
Register the contact email address for each branch. Request result notifications are sent to the address registered here.
Menu: Admin → Branch Email Management
✅ How to Approve / Reject a Transport Form Request
1
Menu → Transport Form Request Management
The list of requests will be displayed.
2
Review the request details
Check the branch name, requested form type, and quantity.
3
Click Approve or Reject
Clicking Approve lets you choose full or partial approval. Clicking Reject requires you to enter a reason.
4
Automatic email notification
As soon as the decision is made, the result is automatically sent to the registered email address of the relevant branch.
13 · Account
Change Your Password
When you log in with the initial password ($eastar12) for the first time, you will be automatically redirected to the password change screen. After that, you must change your password every 180 days (approximately 6 months). If the period has expired, the change screen will appear immediately upon login.
The initial password for new accounts is $eastar12. On first login, you will be forced to change it. You cannot access any other menu until the change is complete.
1
Bottom of left menu → Change Password
Click Change Password at the very bottom of the sidebar. (On first login, you will be taken there automatically.)
2
Enter your current password
Enter the password you are currently using. (If this is your first time, enter $eastar12.)
3
Enter your new password twice
Enter your new password, then enter it again to confirm.
4
Complete the change
Click the Change Password button and the new password takes effect immediately.
If you have forgotten your password, ask your administrator to reset it. After a reset, your password reverts to $eastar12 and you will be forced to change it again.
